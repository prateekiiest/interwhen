 • The X’s form a continuous path: (2,3), (3,3), (4,3), then (5,3), (5,4), (5,5), (4,5).
 • E is at (3,5).

2. Determine the moves between consecutive points:
 • From S (1,3) to (2,3): move Down.
 • From (2,3) to (3,3): move Down.
 • From (3,3) to (4,3): move Down.
 • From (4,3) to (5,3): move Down.
 • From (5,3) to (5,4): move Right.
 • From (5,4) to (5,5): move Right.
 • From (5,5) to (4,5): move Up.
 • From (4,5) to E (3,5): move Up.

3. Identify where the direction changes (i.e., turns):
 • The first four moves are all Down, so no turn.
 • The change from Down to Right (from (5,3) to (5,4)) is a right turn.
 • Continuing Right from (5,4) to (5,5) keeps the same direction.
 • The change from Right to Up (from (5,5) to (4,5)) is a left turn.
 • The final move Up (from (4,5) to (3,5)) continues in the same direction.

Thus, there are exactly 2 turns in the path.
