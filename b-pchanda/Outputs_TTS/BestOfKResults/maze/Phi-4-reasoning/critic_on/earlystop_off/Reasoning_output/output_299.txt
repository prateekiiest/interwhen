Now, comparing consecutive moves:
 • From move 1 (left) to move 2 (left): no turn.  
 • From move 2 (left) to move 3 (down): facing left, a right turn (clockwise) leads to down. → Right turn #1.  
 • From move 3 (down) to move 4 (down): no turn.  
 • From move 4 (down) to move 5 (left): facing down, a right turn leads to left. → Right turn #2.  
 • The remaining transitions do not involve a 90° right turn.

Thus, there are exactly 2 right turns along the path, which corresponds to option B.
