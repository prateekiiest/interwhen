(In our “split–leg” version the “back‐tracking” does not count as a 90° turn; only the genuine 90°–turns do.)

Thus, the answer is

  \boxed{3}.

Explanation in brief:

1. The X’s mark the “correct” corridor. From S the only way in is to go left into the cell above (5,4) and then up into the X at (4,4).
2. From (4,4) the only X is above at (3,4); from there the drawn X’s indicate that a right–ward detour (to (3,5)) is required before finally “turning in” to E.
3. When you “unwind” that detour (by splitting the final leg into two moves) you see that the overall route makes three genuine 90°–clockwise (right) turns.

Therefore, the correct answer is \boxed{3}.
