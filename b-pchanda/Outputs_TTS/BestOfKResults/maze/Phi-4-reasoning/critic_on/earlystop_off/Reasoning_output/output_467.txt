

Comparing each successive move we find that the only changes in direction that qualify as 90° right turns occur when the path goes from rightward to downward (a right turn) and later from downward to leftward (again a right turn). No other segment changes direction by a right‐angle clockwise. Thus, there are 2 right turns along the designated path.
