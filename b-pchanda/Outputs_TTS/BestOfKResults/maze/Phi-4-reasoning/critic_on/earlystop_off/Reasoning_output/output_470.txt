9. (6,4) to (5,4): North  
10. (5,4) to (4,4): North  
11. (4,4) to (4,3): West  
12. (4,3) to (4,2): West  
13. (4,2) to (5,2): South  
14. (5,2) to (6,2): South

Now, we compare consecutive segments to identify right turns (a 90° clockwise change):  
• From East (segment 1) to East (segment 2): No turn.  
• From East (segment 2) to South (segment 3): Turning right (East → South). [Right turn #1]  
• From South (segment 3) to South (segment 4): No turn.  
• From South (segment 5) to South (segment 6): No turn.  
• From South (segment 6) to West (segment 7): Turning right (South → West). [Right turn #2]  
• From West (segment 7) to West (segment 8): No turn.  
• From West (segment 8) to North (segment 9): Turning right (West → North). [Right turn #3]  
• From North (segment 9) to North (segment 10): No turn.  
• From North (segment 10) to West (segment 11): This is a left turn (not counted).  
• From West (segment 11) to West (segment 12): No turn.  
• From West (segment 12) to South (segment 13): This is a left turn (not counted).  
• From South (segment 13) to South (segment 14): No turn.

Thus, there are exactly 3 right turns along the path.
