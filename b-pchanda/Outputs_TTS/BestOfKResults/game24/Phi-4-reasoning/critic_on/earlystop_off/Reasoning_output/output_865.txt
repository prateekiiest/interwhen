  (11×3 – 9) × (8 ÷ (11 – 9÷3)) = 24.

This expression uses the numbers 11, 3, 9, and 8 exactly once and only the operations +, –, ×, and ÷.

Thus, the final answer is

\boxed{(11\times3-9)\times\frac{8}{11-\frac{9}{3}}}
