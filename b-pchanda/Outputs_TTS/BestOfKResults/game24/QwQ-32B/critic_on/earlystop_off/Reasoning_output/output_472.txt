\[
13 \times 3 = 39
\]
Next, subtract 2 from the result:
\[
39 - 2 = 37
\]
Finally, subtract the remaining 13:
\[
37 - 13 = 24
\]

This uses all four numbers (13, 3, 2, and the second 13) exactly once and results in 24. The expression is:

\[
\boxed{13 \times 3 - 2 - 13}
