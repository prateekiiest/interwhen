To solve the Game of 24 with the numbers 1, 4, 8, and 8, we need to use all four numbers exactly once with the operations +, -, *, / to reach exactly 24. 

One valid approach is:
1. Multiply 8 by 4 to get 32.
2. Divide the second 8 by 1 to get 8.
3. Subtract the result of step 2 from the result of step 1: \(32 - 8 = 24\).

This uses all four numbers (8, 4, 8, and 1) exactly once. 

The expression is:
\[
\boxed{8 \times 4 - 8 \div 1}
\]
