   - Narwhal's Novels (NN) is northwest of YY (x < 0.5 and y > –0.5) and northeast of IAS (x > 0 and y > 0). Thus, choose NN = (0.3, 0.5).  
   - Bowling Alley Boutique (BAB) is northwest of Daisy's Dresses (x < 1 and y > –1) and southeast of IAS (x > 0, y < 0). So, BAB = (0.5, –0.2) works well.

4. **Counting Objects to the East of FF:**  
   Objects are to the east of FF if their x-coordinate is greater than –0.5.  
   - IAS: (0, 0) → 0 > –0.5  
   - DD: (1, –1) → 1 > –0.5  
   - YY: (0.5, –0.5) → 0.5 > –0.5  
   - NN: (0.3, 0.5) → 0.3 > –0.5  
   - BAB: (0.5, –0.2) → 0.5 > –0.5  
   - FF itself is not counted.

   This gives a total of 5 objects to the east of Factory Finds.
