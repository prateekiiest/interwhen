However, the statement “Wild Water Park is to the Northwest of Amusement Arcade” is interpreted (when read in the “reverse” sense) as A being southeast of W. Thus, compared to A, W is positioned more to the west and (relatively) more to the north.

3. Ibex Instruments (I) is described as “to the Northwest of Amusement Arcade” and “to the Northeast of Wild Water Park.” In effect, I is placed so that relative to A it is shifted further west and north, and relative to W it is shifted further east and north. In a consistent configuration the natural “middle‐ground” for I ends up being in a position such that when one measures the vector from I to O, the resulting displacement points toward the southwest.

4. A sample consistent configuration (after a suitable reinterpretation of the directional cues) is as follows:  
  • Let O = (0,0).  
  • Choose A = (10, –5) so that A is southeast of O.  
  • Choose W = (–4, –3) so that W is southwest of O and (when viewed from A) W is “northwest‐ish” (i.e. A is southeast of W).  
  • Then choose I = (3, 1) which satisfies being northwest of A (since A – I = (7, –6) points southeast) and northeast of W (since I – W = (7, 4) points northeast).

5. Finally, the displacement from I to O is  
  O – I = (0 – 3, 0 – 1) = (–3, –1).  
A vector with a negative x–component and a negative y–component points to the southwest.

Thus, based on the relative directions provided, Oscar's Office Supplies is to the southwest of Ibex Instruments.
